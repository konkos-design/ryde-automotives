All mechanical repairs

One local workshop for the regular service, the rego check and the jobs that need the car up on the hoist for a while.

  • Servicing and repairs

    General servicing for everyday cars, with a clear heads-up on what is due next.

  • Pink slips

    eSafety checks for your rego renewal, done while you wait.

  • Blue slips

    Inspections to get an unregistered or interstate car on NSW plates.

  • Clutch and gearbox

    Clutch replacements and gearboxes out on the bench when they need it.

  • Steering and suspension

    Steering racks, front end and suspension parts.

  • Engine work

    From diagnosis to pulling the engine down, on everyday and performance cars.

  • LPG

    LPG work, the way it says on the sign.

Pink and blue slips, while you wait

Bring the car in, wait while it's checked, and drive away with the paperwork.

Pink slip

Rego renewal

The eSafety check NSW asks for when you renew the registration on a car more than five years old.

  • Tyres, brakes, lights, steering, seatbelts and more
  • Same-day spots are sometimes available
  • Any work found is explained before it's done

Blue slip

Unregistered cars

The identity and safety inspection for a car that is unregistered, has been off the road, or is coming in from another state.

  • Bring the car and its papers
  • Inspection done on site
  • Call ahead to check the timing
