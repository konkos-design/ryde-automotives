Servicing and repairs

Regular servicing keeps small problems small. The team checks the car over, tells you what is due now and what can wait, and gives you a sensible timeframe for the rest.

  • General car servicing
  • Oil, filters and fluids
  • A heads-up on what is due next
  • No pressure to do it all at once

Pink and blue slips

Pink slips (eSafety checks) for rego renewals and blue slips for unregistered or interstate cars, done on site while you wait. Ring ahead and you may get a same-day spot.

  • Pink slip: eSafety check for registration renewal
  • Blue slip: for unregistered, off-road or interstate vehicles
  • Any failed item explained, with the option to fix it on the spot
  • Call ahead to check the next free slot
Book a slip

Clutch and gearbox

A slipping clutch or a gearbox that crunches into gear is a job for the hoist. Ryde Automotives removes the box, replaces what is worn and can show you photos of the job.

  • Clutch kits and flywheels
  • Gearbox removal and refitting
  • Driveline noises and vibrations
  • Photos of the repair so you can see what was done
Ask about your clutch

Steering and suspension

Heavy steering, knocks over bumps or a car that wanders on the road. Steering racks and suspension parts are part of the everyday work here.

  • Steering rack replacement
  • Steering and front-end faults
  • Suspension repairs
  • Fixes for items that fail a slip
Ask about steering
