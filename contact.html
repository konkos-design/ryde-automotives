Before you come in

Booking a pink or blue slip? Call first to check the next free time. Bring your rego papers, and for a blue slip any paperwork that came with the car.

Car won't drive? Customers have arrived on the back of a tow truck before. Call the workshop and let them know it's coming.

Getting the car back. If the team finds something unexpected, they call you to explain it before carrying on.
